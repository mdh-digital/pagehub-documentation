# Profile & preferences

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar → **Profile**.

## Account information

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Your display name |
| **Email** | Your sign-in address, badged **Verified** or **Email not verified** |
| **Photo** | JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 2 MB |

## Change password

Requires your **current password**, a **new password** and its confirmation. A strength indicator covers two basics: minimum length, and a mix of letters and digits.

Forgotten your current password? Sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. See [Sign up & sign in](/en/getting-started/account-setup).

## Access

The list of organisations your account belongs to, your role in each, and an **Active** marker on the one you have open.

Switch organisations from the account menu in the sidebar — the option only appears if you belong to more than one.

## Language

The language picker is in the app header. Seven options: Indonesian, English, العربية, Deutsch, Español, Tiếng Việt, Français.

What follows it:

- The entire builder interface.
- **Emails from PageHub** — verification codes and password resets use your account language.
- Date and number formatting.

Arabic renders the application right-to-left.

::: info Three different "languages"
| Setting | Affects |
| --- | --- |
| **Account language** (here) | Your dashboard and your emails |
| **[Public site language](/en/website/settings)** | Text PageHub injects into merchant sites — read by your visitors |
| Page content language | Whatever you write, or ask AI for |
:::

## Currency

The currency picker sits next to the language picker. It **only changes how PageHub subscription prices are displayed**, converted using rates maintained by the platform.

Two things it does **not** affect:

- **Payment is still made in Rupiah** for manual transfers. The IDR amount to transfer is always written explicitly on the payment page.
- **Your [store currency](/en/commerce/store-settings)** — that belongs to each website and is never converted.

The default follows your language (id → IDR, en → USD, de/es/fr → EUR, ar → SAR, vi → VND), and you can change it.

## Appearance

The **Tweaks** panel (the palette icon near the account menu) sets accent colour, light/dark, tone, menu style and radius. It's saved to your account, so it follows you to other devices.

If you've never set it, your appearance follows the [team default](/en/team/brand-white-label). Details: [Interface tour](/en/getting-started/interface-tour).

## Signing out

Account menu → **Sign out**. If you signed in with **Remember me**, the session lasts up to 30 days; signing out ends it on that device.
