# Upgrading & payment

## Choosing a plan

**Billing → Upgrade plan**.

<Shot src="/img/account/upgrade-plans.webp" wide>
The Upgrade plan screen: a Monthly / Lifetime switch above the grid, plan cards with prices and quota lists (websites, landing, storage, AI credits/month), a "YOUR PLAN" marker on the active one, and a Summary panel on the right with plan, duration, subtotal, tax and total due.
</Shot>

1. Pick a family: **Monthly** or **Lifetime**.
2. Click **Choose this plan** on the card you want.
3. Pick a **Duration** (for time-limited plans).
4. Check the **Summary** on the right: subtotal, discount, tax, total due, and the validity period.
5. Click **Create transaction**.

You're taken to the payment page.

## Two ways to pay

### Card or PayPal

The **Pay by card or PayPal** card on the payment page. Settles immediately and activates your plan automatically. Accepts cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal.

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record.

### Manual bank transfer

<Shot src="/img/account/payment-manual.webp">
The manual transfer payment page: a destination bank picker, sender bank name, sender account number, transfer amount, a proof-of-transfer uploader, and a "Send proof of transfer" button.
</Shot>

1. Pick a **destination bank**.
2. Transfer the **exact amount** shown.
3. Fill in your bank name, account number and the amount.
4. Upload **proof of transfer** (JPG/PNG, up to 5 MB).
5. Click **Send proof of transfer**.

The status becomes **Awaiting verification**. An admin reviews it manually.

::: warning Transfers are always in Rupiah
If your price display is in another currency, the payment page still writes out the **Rupiah amount to transfer** explicitly. Transfer that number, not the one on the plan card.
:::

## Transaction statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Awaiting payment** | Transaction created, no proof yet |
| **Awaiting verification** | Proof sent, waiting on an admin |
| **Success** | Approved. The plan is active |
| **Rejected** | Declined, with a reason |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled it |

The **Refresh status** button reloads the page. Once approved you'll see a confirmation such as *"Payment approved. Plan active until 30 Sep 2026."*

## Renewal extends, it doesn't replace

If your subscription is still running, a renewal **extends** from the remaining period. You don't lose unused days.

The exception is lifetime plans: moving from lifetime to a time-limited plan **replaces** it and can't be undone. See [Plans & quota](/en/account/plans-and-quota).

## Buying AI credits

**Billing → AI Credit → Buy credits**. Enter the number of units; the price and credits per unit are shown in the dialog. The payment flow is the same.

Details: [AI credits](/en/ai/credits).

## Buying storage

**Billing → Storage Add-On**. Pick a capacity pack and pay through the same flow.

## Add-ons

**Billing → Add-ons** for extra store room and white label. See [Add-ons](/en/account/add-ons).

## Transaction history

Every purchase — plans, credits, storage, add-ons — is recorded in one place: **Transaction history**, with date, type, amount and a link to the invoice.

This includes card payments too: two payment methods, one ledger.

## Cancelling a transaction

Unpaid transactions can be deleted from their detail page or from the history. This can't be undone.

## Display currency

The currency picker in the header changes how plan prices are displayed, using rates maintained by the platform. Symbols and placement follow each currency's conventions.

What you transfer is still Rupiah.
