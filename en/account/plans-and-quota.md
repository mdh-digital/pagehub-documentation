# Plans & quota

Open **Billing** in the sidebar (Business group).

<Shot src="/img/account/billing-hub.webp" wide>
The Billing hub: an active plan status card at the top with quota bars (Websites, Landing, Team, Storage, AI credits), then menu cards: Upgrade plan, AI Credit, Storage Add-On, Add-ons, and AppSumo.
</Shot>

## What the plan limits

| Quota | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Websites** | Number of websites |
| **Landing** | Number of landing pages — **counted separately** from websites |
| **Workspaces** | Number of [workspaces](/en/team/workspaces) |
| **Team** | Number of [members](/en/team/members-and-access) |
| **Storage** | Total file size in the [File Manager](/en/media/file-manager) |
| **AI credits** | The monthly AI usage allowance |

Some plans also cap pages per website, articles per website, and contacts per website per month.

Uncapped quotas show as **Unlimited**.

## Quota vs credits

Easy to confuse:

- **Quota** limits *how many may exist*. Checked whenever you create something.
- **[AI credits](/en/ai/credits)** limit *how much AI you may use*. Spent whenever AI works.

## When a quota is full

When you try to create something beyond the limit, PageHub refuses with a message naming which quota is full, plus two ways out: **upgrade the plan**, or delete something you no longer use.

Because website and landing quotas are separate, a full website quota does **not** stop you creating landing pages.

## Plan-gated features

Beyond numbers, some capabilities switch on or off with your plan:

| Feature | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **[Custom domain](/en/website/custom-domain)** | Subscribers only; trial plans can't |
| **[White label](/en/team/brand-white-label)** | Included in some plans; otherwise available as an [add-on](/en/account/add-ons) |
| **[Online store](/en/commerce/overview)** | Sold as a per-website add-on, separate from plans |

## Plan families

The **Upgrade plan** screen has a switch above the grid:

| Family | How you pay |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | A recurring subscription with an expiry |
| **Lifetime** | Pay once, no expiry |

The switch disappears if either side is empty. The screen opens on the family you're currently on, so your own plan is always visible.

::: info AI credits on lifetime plans
Lifetime plans get credits **once, up front**, into the top-up balance that never expires. There's no monthly refill. When they run out, buy more.
:::

::: warning Leaving a lifetime plan
An organisation has exactly one subscription. Moving from a lifetime plan to a monthly one **replaces it, with no way back** — buying the lifetime plan again means paying again, assuming it's still sold.

PageHub raises a dedicated confirmation dialog for this.
:::

## Adding storage

**Billing → Storage Add-On** sells storage capacity beyond your plan's quota. Some storage packs have a validity period; some have none.

## Validity

The status card shows **Renews &lt;date&gt;** for time-limited subscriptions. Renewal **extends** from the remaining period rather than replacing it — so you never lose unused days.

What happens if a subscription passes its date: [When a subscription lapses](/en/account/subscription-lapse).
