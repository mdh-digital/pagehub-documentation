# Partner Store

A Partner Store is an online store that is **free with no expiry** for merchants in Indonesia. There is no monthly bill. PageHub is paid from a small service fee the buyer pays on each QRIS, VA or e-wallet payment.

## How it differs from a regular store

| | Partner Store |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly cost | None |
| Plan website slot | Not used |
| Product & order limits | None |
| When your plan ends | The store **stays live** and can still be managed |
| How buyers pay | Only QRIS, VA and e-wallets through PageHub. Manual transfer is off |
| Currency | Rupiah only |
| How many | One per workspace. You can have several workspaces |

Your landing pages and other websites still follow your plan.

## Requirements

- The account owner's **identity verification** is approved. See [Online payments & balance](/en/commerce/online-payments).
- Payment through the PageHub gateway is open on the platform. If the screen says it is not open yet, wait until it is; you can still send your identity verification first.
- The store sells in rupiah.

## Turning it on

Open **Billing → Partner Store**. The screen has three steps.

1. **Verify your identity** — send your KTP and a selfie. Once covers every store.
2. **Choose a website**, in one of two ways:
   - Click **Make it a Partner Store** on an existing website, **or**
   - Type a name for a new store and click **Create store**. The new website becomes a Partner Store straight away, uses no plan slot, and can be created **even when your plan has ended**. You are taken to that website to start filling it in.
3. **Your partner stores** — the stores already active, each with **Manage store** and **Remove**.

Once active, add products in **Store → Products**, then [choose the products shown](/en/commerce/sell-on-sites) and build the [storefront](/en/commerce/storefront).

## Removing

A Partner Store website cannot be deleted straight from the website list. Remove it first in **Billing → Partner Store → Remove**, then choose:

- **Just remove** — the website stays as a regular website and starts using a plan slot. Only possible while you have a free slot.
- **Delete website** — the website is deleted for good. Read the warning in the dialog before pressing it. Any balance not yet withdrawn stays in the **Balance** menu.

## When your plan ends

A Partner Store is outside the [active period flow](/en/account/subscription-lapse): it stays live, keeps taking orders, and is never deleted with the rest. Your other websites and landing pages still follow that flow.
