# Partner Store

A Partner Store is an online store that is **free with no expiry** for merchants in Indonesia. There is no monthly bill. PageHub is paid from a small service fee the buyer pays on each QRIS, VA or e-wallet payment.

## How it differs from a regular store

| | Partner Store |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly cost | None |
| Plan website slot | Not used |
| Product & order limits | None |
| When your plan ends | The store **stays live** and can still be managed |
| How buyers pay | Only QRIS, VA and e-wallets through PageHub. Manual transfer and your own gateways are off |
| Currency | Rupiah only |
| How many | One per workspace. You can have several workspaces |

## What is included, and what is not

What is free is **that one store website**: all its pages, the shop pages, cart, checkout, invoices and its blog.

**Landing pages are not included.** A landing page always follows your plan. If your account has no active plan (the trial ended or the subscription stopped), landing pages **do not go live**, even when they sell products from the same store and even when you have a Partner Store. The same goes for your other websites.

::: tip A sales page without a plan
If you are not subscribed and need a promo page for one product, build it **as a page inside the Partner Store website**: open your store website → **Pages** tab → add a page, then drop in product blocks from the **Store** group of the [block palette](/en/editor/blocks). That page is free and goes live with the store.
:::

## Why payments only go through PageHub

A Partner Store is free because every buyer payment goes through the PageHub gateway and carries a service fee. That fee pays for the store. So in a Partner Store:

- **Manual transfer to your bank account is off.** Buyers see no transfer option on the invoice.
- **Your own gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Midtrans, Razorpay and the others) cannot be used.** In **Store → Manage store → Store settings**, the payment gateway section says *"Your own gateways are off for a Partner Store"*.
- Buyers pay by **QRIS, virtual account or e-wallet**. The service fee is added **on top of** the order total and paid by the buyer. You receive the full order total in your [Balance](/en/commerce/online-payments).

If you want your own gateway or manual transfer, the store has to stop being a Partner Store and use a paid plan slot (see [Ending a Partner Store](#ending-a-partner-store)).

## Requirements

- The account owner's **identity verification** is approved. See [Online payments & balance](/en/commerce/online-payments).
- Payment through the PageHub gateway is open on the platform. If the screen says it is not open yet, wait until it is; you can still send your identity verification first.
- The store sells in rupiah.
- That workspace has no Partner Store yet.

## Turning it on

Open **Billing → Partner Store**. The screen has three steps.

1. **Verify your identity**: send your KTP and a selfie. Once covers every store.
2. **Choose a website**, in one of two ways:
   - Click **Make it a Partner Store** on an existing website, **or**
   - Click **Create store** under *Open a new partner store*. The choices are the same as for any new website: **Use a template**, **Build with AI** or **Start blank**. Whichever you pick, the new website is a Partner Store from the start, uses no plan slot, and can be created **even when your plan has ended**.
3. **Your partner stores**: the stores already active, each with **Manage store** and **Request removal**.

Building with AI uses [AI credits](/en/ai/credits) like any website. Templates and blank sites use none.

Once active, add products in **Store → Products**, then [choose the products shown](/en/commerce/sell-on-sites) and build the [storefront](/en/commerce/storefront).

## Ending a Partner Store

A Partner Store cannot be deleted or switched off from the website list, and its shop switch cannot be turned off. Ending one is a **request** that the PageHub team reviews.

1. Open **Billing → Partner Store**.
2. On the store you want to end, click **Request removal**.
3. Choose what happens if it is approved:
   - **Keep as an ordinary website**: the website stays, starts using one website slot on your plan, and manual transfer can be switched on again. Only available while your plan has a free website slot.
   - **Remove and delete the website**: the website's pages, blog and bookings are deleted.
4. Fill in **Why do you want to end it?** (at least 10 characters), then **Send request**.

While you wait, the store keeps running as usual and the Partner Store screen shows the date of your request. You can still **Cancel request**.

The PageHub team approves or rejects it, usually within one working day, and **tells you by email**. If it is rejected, the reason is also shown on the Partner Store screen.

**Whatever the decision, the store's contents stay.** Products, orders and balance belong to the workspace store, not to the website. They remain in the **Store** and **Balance** menus, even if the website is deleted.

## When your plan ends

A Partner Store is outside the [active period flow](/en/account/subscription-lapse): it stays live, keeps taking orders, and is never deleted with the rest. You can still sign in and manage it.

Your other websites and landing pages still follow that flow: offline, then deleted on the date given in the email and the banner.
