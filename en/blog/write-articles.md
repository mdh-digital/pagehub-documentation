# Writing articles

Open a website → the **Blog** tab → **Articles** → **Write article**.

<Shot src="/img/blog/article-editor.webp" wide>

"Write with AI", Cancel and Save sit in the top right; the SEO card is below the body.
</Shot>

## The fields

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Required. The slug is derived from it if you don't set one |
| **Description** | The SEO meta tag — shown in search results and on article list cards |
| **Article body** | Rich text: headings, bold/italic, lists, links, images, quotes |
| **Slug (URL)** | Auto from the title; editable |
| **Category** | From the list on the Categories tab |
| **Thumbnail** | The article card image and the share preview |
| **Keywords** | Type then Enter or comma |

There's also a **"Use this thumbnail as the article page favicon"** checkbox — unticked, the article uses the website favicon.

## Status & scheduling

In the **Publication** panel:

- **Make published** — the article goes live.
- **Make draft** — hidden.
- **Publish at** — pick a future date and time. The article stays hidden until then, and **publishes itself**.

Useful for preparing several articles at once and releasing them gradually.

## Writing with AI

The **Write with AI** button in the article editor.

1. Fill in **What should it be about?** — e.g. `How to pick a villa for a 50-person company retreat`.
2. Click **Write**.

AI writes the **title, description, keywords and body**, and **picks a category** from your existing list. It also reads the website's brand brief, so the tone matches the rest of the site.

::: warning This replaces what's currently there
The title, description, keywords and body in the form will be overwritten. If you've already written something, copy it out first.
:::

Always **review before saving**: AI doesn't know the facts about your business, so figures, names, prices and any claims need checking.

Uses [AI credits](/en/ai/credits).

## AI blog schedules

The **Blog → AI schedule** tab produces articles on a cadence without you writing each one.

<Shot src="/img/blog/blog-schedule.webp">
The AI schedule tab: a table of schedules with Schedule, Progress, Output and Next run columns; a "New schedule" button in the top right. One row shows an Active status with "2 articles every 1 week".
</Shot>

Creating a schedule:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | For you, e.g. `Villa content Q3` |
| **Content pillars & direction** | The brief: what it's about, who for, what tone. The more specific, the less generic the result |
| **Output** | **Save as draft** or **Publish immediately** |
| **Articles per run** | How many each time it runs |
| **Every … (day/week/month)** | The cadence |
| **Start / End** | Optional. Empty = start now / no end date |

Then **Plan titles with AI** to fill the **queue**: the list of titles to be written. You can edit that queue, or write your own titles.

Every run is recorded in the **Log**, so you always know what happened.

Schedules can be **Paused** and **Resumed** at any time. Deleting a schedule removes its queue and log — articles already written stay.

::: tip Start with drafts
For your first schedule, choose **Save as draft**. Read a few results, improve the brief, and only switch to **Publish immediately** once you trust the quality.
:::

Planning titles and writing each article both use AI credits. An estimate is shown in the form before you save the schedule.

## The article list

The **Articles** tab lists every article with its title, category, view count and last-updated time. There's a search box, and a **Scheduled** marker on articles waiting for their publish time.
