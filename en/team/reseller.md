# Reseller

With the reseller feature you sell this builder to your own clients: each client gets their own account and login under **your brand**, and pays for a plan at **the price you set**.

## What you manage

The Reseller page has four tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Clients** | Your clients' accounts, their plans and status |
| **Client plans** | Your own price list: the name, price and limits of each plan |
| **Balance** | Client payments, the platform fee and payouts |
| **Domain** | Your own address for the builder under your brand |

## Creating a client plan

Create a plan before opening your first client.

1. **Client plans** tab → **New plan**.
2. Enter a name and the **price per month**. A yearly price is optional.
3. Set its limits: websites, landing pages, members, AI credits per month, storage, products and orders per month.

Two rules apply on save:

- A client plan's limits **cannot exceed** those of your own plan. The maximum is shown under each field.
- The price **cannot be below the platform's minimum fee**.

## Opening a client account

1. **Clients** tab → **New client**.
2. Enter the client's business name, the owner's name and email, and pick a plan.
3. **Create client**.

The client gets an email to set a password. The account is active straight away on a **trial** of a few days; its length is shown in the figures at the top.

You are made an admin of that client's account automatically. **Open** switches you into it so you can work on their site.

The number of clients is limited by your plan's **slots**. The "Client slots" figure shows how many are in use.

## What a client sees

- The builder under **your name and logo**, not PageHub's. Set them in [Brand & white label](/en/team/brand-white-label).
- A **Plan** menu holding only your plans. The client picks one and pays on the same invoice screen every other payment uses.
- Clients do not see PageHub's prices and cannot change the brand.

## Balance and payouts

Every client payment is listed on the **Balance** tab with three numbers: what the client paid, the platform fee, and your share.

- The **platform fee** is a percentage of each payment, with a minimum amount per client per month. Both are shown at the top of the Reseller page.
- Your share is **held** for the refund period before it can be withdrawn. The length of the hold is shown on the balance card.
- **Request payout** requests everything that is available. Enter where to send it; the PageHub team transfers it and marks it paid.

## Your own domain

On the **Domain** tab you connect an address such as `app.youragency.com`. Clients sign in there and the sign-in page already carries your brand.

1. Type the domain, **Add domain**.
2. Create the two DNS records shown: a **TXT** record proving the domain is yours, and a **CNAME** pointing the address here.
3. **Check again** until the status is **Connected**. DNS changes can take a few hours.

There is no self-signup on your domain. Client accounts are always opened by you from the Clients tab.

## Lifetime accounts

If your account is a lifetime one, your clients can only **rent** (monthly or yearly). One-time plans for clients are not available.

## If your subscription ends

During the grace period your clients are not affected. If the grace period passes without renewal, client accounts are **taken over by PageHub**: their sites and content stay as they are, the period they already paid for runs to its end, and they are told by email. See [If your subscription ends](/en/account/subscription-lapse).
