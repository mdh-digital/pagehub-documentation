# Landing page templates

A landing page template is **one finished page** you can copy and adapt. Different from a [website template](/en/website/website-templates), which brings an entire site.

## Using a template

From **Landing Page** → **New landing page** → **Use a template**, or via the **Templates** menu in the sidebar.

<Shot src="/img/landing/template-gallery.webp" wide>
The landing page template gallery: a grid of cards with thumbnails, names and categories; category filters and a search box at the top; Detail and Preview buttons on each card.
</Shot>

- **Preview** shows the original page.
- **Use** copies it into a new landing page of your own.

Images inside the template are **copied into your organisation's [File Manager](/en/media/file-manager)** and the links are rewritten to point at those copies — so changes to the original template can't affect your page.

Once copied, the page is entirely yours.

## Saving a page as a template

From the editor: ⋯ → **Save as template**.

It asks for a name, an optional description, a category and a thumbnail. Templates you save are **private** — only your organisation sees them.

Useful when you have a campaign skeleton that works and you just swap the copy for each new promotion.

## Visibility

| Visibility | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Private** | Your organisation only |
| **Global** | All PageHub users — maintained by the platform team |

## Categories

Templates are grouped by category (business, events, digital products, services, and so on). Categories are managed by the platform; you just pick one when saving.

## Previewing without an account

The global template gallery is also browsable from the PageHub marketing site, previews included, without signing in. Handy when you want to show a client the options before they have an account.
