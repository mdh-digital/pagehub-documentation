# Writing prompts

The quality of the page you get is almost entirely decided by what you type into the prompt box. This page is about how to type it.

## The basic formula

A good prompt answers four things:

1. **What this is** — the kind of business or campaign.
2. **Who it's for** — the audience.
3. **How it should feel** — visual impression and tone of voice.
4. **What's in it** — the sections you want.

A complete example:

> A home bakery called "Sweet Cakes" in Bandung. We sell birthday cakes and custom orders for families. It should feel warm and sweet, pastel colours, friendly copy. I need a hero with a cake photo, a catalogue of 6 signature products, customer testimonials, a short story about the owner, and a WhatsApp ordering button.

Compare with:

> make a cake shop website

The second still produces something — but something generic, and you'll spend more credits fixing it than you saved by typing less.

## Worth mentioning

| Mention | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Colours** | "deep green and cream", "monochrome with an orange accent" |
| **Tone** | "casual and friendly", "formal and reassuring" |
| **Sections** | "hero, 3 pricing tiers, FAQ, contact form" |
| **The call to action** | "the main button should say 'Order on WhatsApp'" |
| **Content language** | "write the copy in English" |
| **What to avoid** | "no stock photos of office workers" |

## If you have a reference

In [AI chat](/en/editor/ai-chat) you can attach images — a screenshot of a site you like, product photos, or a colour palette. Say explicitly what you want from it:

> Use the colour scheme from this image, but keep the layout as it is now.

Image attachments depend on the AI provider on your installation; if the attach button isn't there, the mode isn't available.

## Revision prompts

For changing an existing page, three rules make results far more accurate:

**1. Name the section.**

- ✅ `in the pricing section, change the button to "Try free"`
- ❌ `change the button`

**2. One intent per request.** Three short requests are cheaper, faster and miss less often than one long request changing five things.

**3. Say what's wrong, not just what you want.**

- ✅ `the hero is too tall, on a laptop the button is below the fold`
- ❌ `fix the hero`

## Copy content

AI writes plausible placeholder copy, but it doesn't know the facts about your business. Prices, phone numbers, addresses, product names and any figures **must be checked and corrected by you**.

The fastest route: put the important facts in the prompt.

> Plan prices: Basic $12, Pro $29, Business $59 per month. WhatsApp 0812-3456-7890.

## Context that's already included

For pages **inside a website**, AI always reads the website name, description, brand brief and theme — so you don't have to repeat "cake shop in Bandung, pastel colours" every time. See [Theme & brand](/en/website/theme-and-brand).

For **landing pages**, nothing is included automatically. All the direction has to be in the prompt.

## Language

Write your prompt in whichever language you're comfortable with. If the page copy needs to be in a different language than your prompt, say so: "write the page copy in English."
