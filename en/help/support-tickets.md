# Support tickets

**Support** in the sidebar (Workspace group). Report a problem or request a feature; replies come back in the same thread.

## Opening a ticket

Click **New ticket** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Subject** | One line |
| **Details** | What happened, and what you expected |
| **Kind** | **Support** (something is broken) or **Feature request** (something you'd like the product to do) |
| **Priority** | Low · Normal · High |

## What gets a ticket resolved quickly

Write these three things in **Details**:

1. **What you did** — which page, which button, in what order.
2. **What happened** — the error message, verbatim.
3. **What you expected.**

Also include:

- **The page address** — both the dashboard URL and the public one.
- **When** it happened.
- **Browser and device.**
- **A screenshot** if you have one.

The single biggest cause of back-and-forth: "my page is broken" without saying which page.

## Replying

Open the ticket, write in the reply box, click **Send reply**. Replies from the PageHub team are marked **Staff**.

The whole conversation stays in one thread — no history scattered across email.

## Feature requests

Pick the **Feature request** kind for something that doesn't exist yet. These go to a different board than fault reports, so they don't sink beneath urgent tickets.

Include **the problem you're trying to solve**, not just the solution you have in mind — there's often a faster route you don't know about yet.

## Before opening a ticket

Check [Common problems](/en/help/troubleshooting) first — most incoming questions are already answered there.
