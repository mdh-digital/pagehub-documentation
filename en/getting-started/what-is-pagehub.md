# What is PageHub

PageHub is where you build and run your business's online presence: **multi-page websites**, **landing pages** for campaigns, an **online store**, plus the **leads** and **reports** that come with them — all from one dashboard, without hopping between apps.

What sets it apart from an ordinary page builder: pages are **assembled by AI from a description you write**, then refined by you in a visual editor or directly in the HTML. You never start from a blank canvas — you start from a page that already exists.

## What you can build

| | Best for | What makes it different |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Website** | Company profiles, catalogues, stores, client sites | Many pages under one address, sharing a header, footer, menu and theme |
| **Landing page** | Promotions, ad campaigns, webinars, pre-orders | A single standalone page with its own styling, quick to produce |

Both use the same editor. The only real difference is scope — [more in Core concepts](/en/getting-started/concepts).

## What PageHub handles for you

Once a page is published, several things run on their own:

- **Hosting and a public address.** Every workspace gets its own address; pages go live without any server setup.
- **Custom domains.** Point your own domain at a site through DNS verification. See [Custom domain](/en/website/custom-domain).
- **Technical SEO.** `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `feed.xml`, canonical tags, Open Graph, and JSON-LD structured data are generated from your own content. See [Search & AI discovery](/en/seo/search-and-ai-discovery).
- **Forms into leads.** Any form on a published page saves its submissions straight into your leads pipeline.
- **Statistics.** Page views, daily traffic, conversions and sales are recorded automatically.

## Who it's for

- **Business owners** who want a site and a store running without hiring a developer.
- **Freelancers and agencies** juggling many clients — each client gets its own [workspace](/en/team/workspaces), and the product can carry your brand instead of ours through [white label](/en/team/brand-white-label).
- **Marketing teams** who need a new landing page every week without queueing behind a web team.
