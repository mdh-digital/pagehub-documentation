# Interface tour

Once you know where things live, the rest is easy. Every PageHub screen has three parts: the **sidebar** on the left, the **page content** in the middle, and **helper panels** that appear in context.

## The sidebar

### Workspace picker

Right below the logo. Click to switch workspace — **the whole screen follows**: the website list, landing pages and File Manager all narrow to that workspace.

Your choice is remembered by the browser, so tomorrow you come back to the same workspace. If that workspace is deleted or your access is revoked, PageHub falls back to another workspace you're still allowed to open.

### Workspace group

| Menu | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Summary, "Continue" (last edited website) and "Needs attention" |
| **Websites** | Every website in the active workspace |
| **Landing Page** | Standalone landing pages |
| **Leads** | Every form submission that arrives |
| **File Manager** | Images and media files, with folders |
| **Block List** | Block troublesome visitor IPs |
| **Support** | Send a ticket to the PageHub team |

### Business group

| Menu | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Billing** | Active plan, quota usage, upgrades, add-ons, invoice history |
| **Workspaces** | Create and manage workspaces, decide who belongs to each |
| **Brand** | Replace the PageHub brand with yours, and set the team's default appearance |
| **Team** | Invite members, set roles and per-website access |

**Brand** and **Team** only appear for organisation owners and admins.

### Account menu

At the very bottom of the sidebar: your name and email. Click it to open **Profile**, **switch organisation** (shown only if you belong to more than one), and **Sign out**.

## Tweaks — the appearance panel

The palette icon near the account menu opens the **Tweaks** panel: a full-height drawer from the right (from the left when Arabic is active).

Five things you can set:

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Accent colour** | Buttons and active markers throughout the app |
| **Light / dark** | Display mode |
| **Tone** | Warmth of the neutral colours (cool / neutral / warm) |
| **Menu style** | Sidebar with labels + icons, or icons only |
| **Radius** | How rounded cards and buttons are |

<Shot src="/img/getting-started/tweaks-panel.webp">
The Tweaks panel open on the right: accent colour swatches, light/dark toggle, tone options, menu style, a radius slider, and a "Show offers" switch.

The important bit: these are **saved to your account, not just your browser**. Open PageHub on another laptop and your appearance comes with you.

And there are two layers:

1. If you have **ever** set it yourself, your personal choice wins.
2. If you **never** have, you follow the *team default* the owner sets on the [Brand](/en/team/brand-white-label) page. When the agency changes its house style, your appearance changes with it.

Owners see a **Make this the team default** button; anyone with their own choice sees **Follow the team default** to hand control back.

## Rail mode

Click the button at the edge of the sidebar to collapse it into a **rail** — icons only. Menu names stay readable to screen readers, and reappear briefly when you hover over the rail.

On small screens the sidebar becomes a drawer opened from the menu button.

## Language & currency

Two pickers sit side by side in the header:

- **Language** — 7 options: Indonesian, English, العربية (right-to-left), Deutsch, Español, Tiếng Việt, Français.
- **Currency** — this only changes **how prices are displayed**. Payment is still made in Rupiah. See [Upgrading & payment](/en/account/billing).

## Breadcrumbs

Above the page content there's always a trail, e.g. `Dashboard › Website › Sweet Cakes`. Click any part to go up a level.
