# Form builder

The form builder makes forms that stand on their own: design one once, then use it on any page, share it as a link, or embed it on another site. Every answer lands on one [lead board](/en/leads/pipeline).

## Creating a form

1. **New form**, give it a name.
2. Add fields from the left panel.
3. **Save**.

<Shot src="/img/leads/form-builder.webp" wide>
The form builder: field types on the left, the form canvas in the middle with its sections and fields, the selected field's settings on the right (label, placeholder, help text, required), and the Settings and Share & embed tabs at the top.
</Shot>

Field types:

| Type | For |
| --- | --- |
| Text, Long text | Free answers, short or long |
| Email, Phone, Number, Date | Answers whose format is checked |
| Dropdown, Single choice, Checkboxes | Picking from a list you define |
| File upload | A photo or document from the visitor |

Each field can have a label, placeholder, help text and a **required** mark. The options of a dropdown, single choice or checkbox field are edited in their own window; type them one by one or paste a whole list.

## Sections and layout

Fields are grouped into **sections**. There are two layouts:

- **Single page**: every section is shown in order, each under its title.
- **Steps**: one section per step, with Next and Back buttons. Good for long forms.

## Three ways to use a form

| Way | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **A block on a page** | The form appears on your website or landing page |
| **Its own link** | The form opens at its own address, for sharing in chat, email or social media |
| **Embed** | The form is placed on another site with one snippet; its height follows its content |

The link and the embed code are on the **Share & embed** tab.

### Placing a form on a page

In the [editor](/en/editor/blocks), click the **Form** block in the palette. A picker opens: choose an existing form, or type a name to create a new one. Creating a form takes you to the form builder, and **Back to editor** returns you to the page you came from.

<Shot src="/img/leads/form-picker.webp">

A placed form block can be clicked on the canvas. The Attributes panel shows the form's name, its field and lead counts, and **Edit form**, **Leads** and **Change form** buttons. A form on a page always shows its latest version; changing the form does not require reopening the page.

A form on a page follows your site's colours and fonts.

## After sending

On the **Settings** tab you choose what a visitor sees after sending: a **message** you write, or a **redirect to another page**.

## Answers and the lead board

Each form has its own lead board. Every answer becomes a card, and the card records **where it came from**: the site and page name when the form sits on a PageHub page, or the outside site's address when it came through an embed.

## Files uploaded by visitors

Visitor files are stored **privately**. They have no public address; only signed-in team members can download them from the lead card.

- Accepted types: images (JPG, PNG, WebP) and documents (PDF, DOCX, XLSX).
- Up to 10 MB per file and 5 files per field.
- Their size counts towards the account's storage quota.

## Switching a form off

An inactive form stops accepting answers. A form block on a page shows nothing while its form is off, and the Attributes panel in the editor warns you.
