# Website settings

The **Settings** tab holds everything that applies to the **whole** website — address, appearance, menu, SEO, and the delete button.

## General settings

### Name

What you see on the dashboard and on website cards. It doesn't appear on public pages — that's the job of the SEO title and the header content.

### Address

The part that appears in the public URL. Lowercase letters, digits, single hyphens.

::: warning Changing the address kills old links
Every link to the old address stops working, including anything Google has indexed. Only change it before the site is out in the world.
:::

### Thumbnail

The image shown on the website card on your dashboard. For you only — visitors never see it. For the preview image when a link is shared on social media, use **OG Image** in the SEO section.

### Public site language

This is **not** your app language. It's the language of the text PageHub injects around your content on public pages: blog titles and widgets, pagination buttons, month names, 404/500 error pages, and the shop text: the built-in shop pages, cart, checkout, [public invoice](/en/commerce/public-invoice) and order emails to buyers.

If your buyers see the invoice or checkout in the wrong language, this is the setting to change, not your account language.

Why it's separate: one agency can run a German client site and an Arabic one while using the dashboard in Indonesian. Arabic also renders those pages right-to-left automatically.

## Global theme

Primary colour, background, text colour and font for the whole website. See [Theme & brand](/en/website/theme-and-brand).

## Default SEO

The starting values for **every new page** in this website. Existing pages don't change, and every page can still have its own SEO through the **SEO & Appearance** menu in the editor.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The browser tab title and the headline in search results |
| **Description** | The summary under the headline in search results |
| **Keywords** | Type then press Enter or comma |
| **OG Image** | The preview image when the link is shared on WhatsApp/Facebook/X |
| **Favicon URL** | The small icon in the browser tab |

The last two can be picked from the **File Manager**.

## Blog appearance

Appears when the blog module is on. Controls the **sidebar** (none / left / right), which widgets show (search, categories, latest articles), and the share buttons on article pages.

A small note: Instagram has no share-by-URL, so what's offered for Instagram is **Copy link**.

## Navigation menu

The menu items that fill the header. See [Navigation menu](/en/website/navigation-menu).

## Tracking

Google Analytics, GTM, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel and a custom head script. See [Tracking & pixels](/en/website/tracking).

## Custom domain

Point your own domain here. See [Custom domain](/en/website/custom-domain).

## Templates & export

Two things:

- **Save as template** — store this entire website (pages, shell, theme, menu, media) as a template you can use to start new websites. See [Website templates](/en/website/website-templates).
- **Download ZIP** — take a copy of your site's HTML.

## Danger zone

**Delete website** removes the website and **all of its pages**. PageHub asks for confirmation and names how many pages go with it. This can't be undone.
