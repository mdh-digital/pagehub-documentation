# Website templates

A website template is a **snapshot of an entire website**: every page, the header and footer, theme, menu, blog settings, SEO, and the media inside it. Use one to start a new website without rebuilding from scratch.

Different from a [landing page template](/en/landing-pages/landing-templates), which is a single page.

## Using a template

From **Websites** → **New website** → **Use a template**.

<Shot src="/img/website/template-gallery.webp" wide>
The website template gallery: cards with thumbnails, template names and categories, category filters at the top, and a search box. Each card has Detail and Preview buttons.
</Shot>

- **Preview** opens a live preview of the site; you can navigate between its pages.
- **Use** copies the template into a new website of your own.

What happens on use:

1. A new website is created, with all its pages.
2. Theme, menu, brand, header/footer and blog settings come along.
3. Images inside the template are **copied into your organisation's [File Manager](/en/media/file-manager)**, and the HTML is rewritten to point at those copies. So even if the original template is deleted, your site is intact.
4. Your website quota decreases by one.

After that it's all yours — change anything.

## Saving a website as a template

Handy for agencies: build one good "recipe" site, then reuse it for client after client.

From the website page, click **Save as template** (or via **Settings** → **Templates & export**).

<Shot src="/img/website/save-as-template.webp">
The "Save as template" dialog: Template name, optional Description, a Category dropdown, a Thumbnail uploader, and a note that this template is private to your organisation.
</Shot>

What it asks for:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Template name** | The name shown in the gallery |
| **Description** | Optional |
| **Category** | From the platform's category list |
| **Thumbnail** | The gallery card image |

## Visibility

| Visibility | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Private** | Your organisation only |
| **Global** | All PageHub users |

Templates you save are always **private**. Global templates are provided and maintained by the platform team.

## What travels and what doesn't

**Copied:**

- Every page and its HTML
- Header & footer (shell)
- Global theme, navigation menu, brand brief
- Blog settings and appearance
- Default SEO
- The images and media those pages use

**Not copied:**

- Custom domain
- Tracking and pixel settings
- Blog articles
- Products, orders and store settings
- Leads and form submissions
- Statistics and view counts

The reason is the same for all of them: that's data belonging to one specific site, not part of its design.

## Download ZIP

**Settings** → **Templates & export** also offers **Download ZIP** so you can take a copy of your site's HTML out of PageHub. Useful as an archive, or when handing files to a client.
