# Booking (appointments)

Booking lets your site take appointments: consultations, haircuts, classes, room hire. Visitors pick a free date and time, then fill in their name and phone number.

Payment is not included. Service prices are shown only, not charged.

## Turning it on

1. Open **Websites**, pick the website, then open the **Booking** tab.
2. Click **Turn it on**.

Once on, booking works straight away with a default schedule: Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00, 30-minute appointments, one person per slot. Adjust it in the four sections below.

The booking page is live at `…/booking` on your site, with that site's header, footer and styling. **Open booking page** opens it.

## The four sections

### Schedule

The appointments that came in, with the status **Scheduled**, **Attended**, **No-show** or **Cancelled**. Mark attendance or cancel from here. Cancelling frees the slot at once, so someone else can book it.

**Add appointment** records a booking made by phone. Staff may enter a time outside opening hours, but cannot overrule a slot that is already full.

### Services

Switch on **Offer a list of services** in Settings when visitors need to pick a service. Each service has a **Length**, **People per slot**, **Price** (shown only) and **Description**.

### Staff & hours

Each person has **Working hours** per day, the services they **Can deliver**, and **Time off**. Someone with no working hours cannot be booked yet.

Switch on **Let visitors pick a person** if visitors may choose their stylist or doctor. When it is off, everything goes into one calendar.

### Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Timezone** | Opening hours are read in this zone, including across daylight saving changes |
| **Ask for a time** | Switch off for all-day services; visitors only pick a date |
| **How times are offered** | A row of start times, or any start time within opening hours |
| **Appointment length** / **Gap after** | The gap is for preparation and is not shown to visitors |
| **People per slot** | 1 for one-to-one appointments, more than 1 for classes |
| **Shortest notice** / **Furthest ahead** | How soon and how far ahead people may book |
| **Intro on the booking page** / **Message after booking** | Text on the booking page |

## A booking block on other pages

The booking form can also be placed on any page from the block palette in the editor. The block appears in the palette once booking is ready to use (switched on and with working hours).

## Double bookings

Two people cannot book the same slot, even in the same second. For classes with several seats, booking stops exactly when the seats run out.
